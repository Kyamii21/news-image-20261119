A newspaper-front-page infographic summing up the world news of
Thursday, 19 November 2026, in a single 1024×1024 image.

**Stories covered**

- **Lead — The big three meet in Shenzhen:** Trump, Xi and Putin hold
  the first-ever three-way summit, with Ukraine, the Iran war, nuclear
  arms and AI on the table; Europe's allies answer with a meeting in
  Berlin the same day.
- **Gulf:** Iran hits US sites in Kuwait — the first attack on Kuwait
  since September — and Bahrain foils a third Iranian attack in a week.
- **Ukraine:** The Crimean Bridge is hit for the third time this week;
  traffic halts hours before Putin's summit in Shenzhen.
- **Business:** Anthropic prices a record $81.3bn IPO at $83 a share;
  trading starts today on Nasdaq as ANT.
- **Culture:** A Van Gogh sells for a record $190.4m at Sotheby's after
  a Nazi-era ownership dispute is settled.
- **Games:** Grand Theft Auto VI begins its global rollout in New
  Zealand; early reviews score 99 on Metacritic.
- **Space:** NASA confirms Voyager 1 is one light-day from Earth —
  16 billion miles away, a radio signal now takes a full day to reach it.

Every headline, name and number was verified against at least two
reputable sources (Reuters, NYT, BBC, NASA, AP, CNN) on the day.
The image is drawn entirely with PIL — no AI-generated text — so every
word stays crisp and legible.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the image to `/output/entry.png`. It uses only the Python
standard library, needs no network, and produces byte-identical output
on every run.
